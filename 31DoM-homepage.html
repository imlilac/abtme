31 days of music is going to be a yearly event I hold in december where I re-listen to albums that have come out during the year, and once a day, I will write a little post regarding the album, my rating of it, and any other thoughts I have. The albums will be rated based on some arbitrary criteria from 0-20, and given a final score out of 100.


These critera are:

  • Songwriting
  • Production
  • Vibes
  • Originality
  • Replay Value

I will be biased, but it's impossible to not be biased, and it's my opinionated list so idc :3
